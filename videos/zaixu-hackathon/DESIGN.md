# 再续西游 Hackathon Video Design

## Overview

The video inherits the game's existing visual identity instead of introducing a separate promo aesthetic. Its anchor is the fiery menu key art: mythic Chinese architecture, ember-lit clouds, parchment surfaces, vermilion seals, and toy-like Journey to the West characters. Gameplay remains crisp and recognizable inside the original 16:9 canvas, while title moments use cinematic crops, fast camera pushes, ink-brush typography, and restrained HUD overlays. The result should feel like a lost browser-game memory being reignited with modern AI and multiplayer systems.

## Colors

- **Night Ink**: `#3B2217` - darkest warm neutral for title frames and vignette edges.
- **Deep Ember**: `#602E1C` - secondary dark surface and shadow tint.
- **Burnt Umber**: `#83442D` - panel borders, supporting labels, and warm midtones.
- **Vermilion**: `#C55C36` - primary action accent, seals, and impact flashes.
- **Flame Orange**: `#E48F4F` - energy trails, transition light, and highlighted words.
- **Scroll Gold**: `#F1BE82` - parchment UI and secondary text.
- **Cloud White**: `#F9D6A4` - highest-value text and luminous edges.
- **Shell Black**: `#0B0A0D` - letterbox, final fade, and deep contrast.

## Typography

- **Display Calligraphy**: QiuHongKai Regular. Hero statements, the project name, and chapter labels. Use at 96-190px with generous breathing room.
- **Brush Accent**: MaShanZheng Regular. Short impact words only, such as "联机", "炼制", and "开打". Never use for paragraphs.
- **Interface Sans**: PingFang SC Semibold / system sans fallback. Supporting copy, feature labels, and technical proof at 24-42px.
- **Data Voice**: Menlo Bold. Room codes, live status, build labels, and compact proof tags at 20-30px with tabular numerals.

## Elevation

Depth comes from real imagery and layered motion, not generic cards. Full-bleed key art uses a dark warm vignette; gameplay is framed by thin scroll-gold edges with a subtle ember bloom and no oversized rounded corners. Small HUD labels may use translucent ink-black plates with a 1px `#83442D` border. Shadows stay warm and dense rather than blue or neutral gray.

## Components

- **Burning Scroll Title**: parchment or dark-ink field with a vermilion seal, brush title, floating ember flecks, and a slow camera push.
- **Gameplay Window**: true 16:9 capture, cropped minimally, edged in scroll gold, with motion continuing inside the footage.
- **Feature Stamp**: compact Chinese label that lands like a seal over footage; maximum two words.
- **System Proof Rail**: a narrow bottom rail for factual callouts such as "装备影响属性" or "链接直达房间".
- **Split-Realm Frame**: two gameplay windows for co-op, separated by a luminous vertical brush stroke rather than a dashboard divider.
- **Forge Flash**: furnace UI, material icons, and weapon result converging into one bright impact frame.
- **Final Portal Mark**: the project name and URL over the login key art, with a red circular CTA echoing the in-game "入界" button.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do's

- Keep the homepage key art visible in the opening and closing beats.
- Let gameplay occupy at least 70% of every feature beat.
- Use warm optical effects: embers, overexposure, firelight, ink wipes, and gold edge glints.
- Preserve the original game UI and pixel-art proportions; the video should prove a real playable build.
- Use short, declarative Chinese text that can be read within two seconds.

### Don'ts

- Do not use cyan, purple-blue gradients, neon grids, or generic cyberpunk styling.
- Do not turn the video into a card-based SaaS explainer.
- Do not blur or darken gameplay so heavily that judges cannot inspect it.
- Do not use unrelated stock footage or generated imagery that changes the characters' established look.
- Do not place long paragraphs on screen or explain controls with visible tutorial copy.
