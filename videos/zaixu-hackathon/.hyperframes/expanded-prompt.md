# 《再续西游》Hackathon Demo V2 Expanded Production Prompt

## Style Block

- Duration: 60 seconds, 1920x1080, 60 fps.
- Mood: a lost browser-game memory reignited as a present-tense playable world.
- Palette: Night Ink `#3B2217`, Deep Ember `#602E1C`, Burnt Umber `#83442D`, Vermilion `#C55C36`, Flame Orange `#E48F4F`, Scroll Gold `#F1BE82`, Cloud White `#F9D6A4`, Shell Black `#0B0A0D`.
- Type: QiuHongKai for hero statements, MaShanZheng for one- or two-word brush accents, PingFang SC for proof labels, Menlo Bold for room and build metadata.
- Visual proof rule: real gameplay occupies at least 70 percent of every feature beat. No label may claim behavior that is not visible in the shot beneath it.
- Concept angle: the film behaves like a burning scroll that opens portals into real gameplay evidence.

## Rhythm

`HOOK-hold-COMBAT-SKILL-FORGE-COOP-PEAK-breathe-CTA`

The energy climbs from memory to proof. Skill impact is the first peak; real two-player combat is the highest peak; the final six seconds hold long enough for public links to be read.

## Global Composition Rules

- Preserve the existing warm mythic-Chinese homepage language. Do not introduce cyberpunk blue, neon grids, generic gradients, or SaaS cards.
- Full-bleed key art provides background depth in opening and close. Gameplay scenes use a scroll-gold frame, warm vignette, ember glints, registration marks, chapter metadata, and one factual proof rail.
- Reuse the existing vermilion ink wipe as the primary transition. Use warm overexposure flashes as accents on skill impact, forge success, and the handoff into co-op battle.
- Keep overlays outside game HUD, dialogue, damage numbers, player nameplates, and inventory result areas.
- Each gameplay beat has two focal points: the recorded action and one short edge-anchored proof statement.

## Scene 1: Memory Can Fight Back, 0-5.5s

**Concept:** The menu key art is not a museum piece. It slowly pushes toward the viewer until the title stamps into place like a portal being opened.

**Depth:** BG full-bleed menu art and warm vignette; MG brush title and hook; FG build metadata, vermilion seal, gold rule, seven drifting embers.

**Choreography:** Camera PUSHES; title ASSEMBLES from the left; seal STAMPS; embers DRIFT upward; the gold rule DRAWS across the lower third.

**Transition:** Vermilion wipe, 0.22s, `power3.inOut`.

## Scene 2: Rebuilt For The Browser, 5.5-12s

**Concept:** Fast proof that this is a real browser game. Login, character identity, and world map arrive as three connected windows into the rebuilt runtime.

**Depth:** BG parchment-toned captures; MG login, Wukong selection, moving map; FG `旧资源 / 原始关卡 / 现代浏览器`, corner crop marks, chapter number.

**Choreography:** Login SLIDES; character frame PUNCHES in on the narration name; map PUSHES forward; metadata TYPES ON.

**Transition:** Warm flash into combat, 0.18s.

## Scene 3: Combat Becomes Skill, 12-22s

**Concept:** Start with physical staff rhythm, then prove the deeper system by releasing a learned active skill into a live monster group. The viewer must see the key-bound skill icon, MP movement, full animation, impact effect, and damage in one continuous shot.

**Depth:** BG live L1 capture; MG Wukong and monsters; FG `技能实战`, `法力`, `冷却`, a compact Y-key marker, thin gold impact bars, chapter stamp.

**Choreography:** Normal attacks CUT on percussion; skill label STAMPS; fire effect EXPANDS; damage PUNCHES upward; proof words LOCK IN one by one.

**Transition:** Weapon-direction brush wipe, 0.24s.

## Scene 4: Ask Laojun, Receive Equipment, 22-35s

**Concept:** The sequence starts on backpack attributes, moves to the Laojun drawer, shows the player's natural-language request, then holds through material and soul consumption until the crafted weapon is visibly present in inventory.

**Depth:** BG furnace recipe surface and portrait; MG player message, Laojun response, forge toast, backpack result; FG `材料扣除 · 装备入包`, material count, soul count, weapon icon, warm forge sparks.

**Choreography:** Gear values COUNT; Laojun drawer SLIDES from the right; the request TYPES ON; furnace light PULSES; result icon CRASHES into the proof rail; final backpack frame HOLDS.

**Transition:** Forge overexposure burn, 0.25s.

## Scene 5: A Link Becomes A Teammate, 35-48s

**Concept:** Room creation is connective tissue, not the payoff. The payoff is two independently authenticated Wukong instances, distinguished by their username nameplates, moving and attacking inside the same L1 battle.

**Depth:** BG live lobby and battle captures; MG host, guest, monsters, damage effects; FG room code, `链接直达`, `双人同场`, two tiny LIVE indicators, scroll-gold divider.

**Choreography:** Room code TYPES ON; share confirmation CLICKS; guest name LOCKS into the member list; hard cut to battle; two players MOVE from opposite sides and attacks CROSS at the monster.

**Transition:** Warm flash followed by a short vermilion wipe, 0.24s.

## Scene 6: The Loop Is Playable, 48-54s

**Concept:** Nine Heavens and Tiangongdao sit side by side as proof that combat, skills, equipment, forging, and co-op form one loop rather than isolated demos.

**Depth:** BG two chapter arts; MG factual loop counters; FG registration marks, chapter names, one circular route line, ember dust.

**Choreography:** Chapter panels PUSH from opposite sides; six loop words ASSEMBLE around the route; the center seal STAMPS on `可玩循环`.

**Transition:** Slow warm focus pull into the title card, 0.5s.

## Scene 7: Public Portal, 54-60s

**Concept:** Return to the homepage key art and hold the project name, play URL, source URL, and final statement without visual competition.

**Depth:** BG final key art, vignette, warm grade; MG project title and tagline; FG PLAY/CODE metadata, final seal, gold rule, drifting embers.

**Choreography:** Title SLIDES in and HOLDS; seal STAMPS; links DRAW upward; embers DRIFT; the whole frame gently BREATHES before fading to Shell Black.

## Recurring Motifs

- Vermilion seal = confirmed product truth.
- Scroll-gold rule = movement between systems.
- Warm overexposure = a game action creating a result.
- Menlo metadata = public, inspectable implementation evidence.

## Negative Prompt

- No static furnace illustration standing in for a forge result.
- No room lobby standing in for multiplayer gameplay.
- No debug-only VFX standing in for a learned skill.
- No regional-accent or stylized system TTS.
- No tiny labels over the game HUD, dialogue, inventory item, or player nameplates.
- No unsupported characters, equipment classes, or gameplay claims.
